# CT-PL-193 v9 (NAC) — Clinical Review Questions

These questions are derived from the open Issues and Phase 5 relation review.
They are for qualified clinical reviewers; no answer is proposed here.

## Source conflicts

1. **BUN threshold for blood cultures and ICU/UCE criteria.** The narrative
   states BUN > 30 mg/dL; the visually inspected flowchart shows BUN > 20.
   Which source representation should prevail for each rule?

2. **ICU/UCE qualification structure.** The narrative defines direct severe
   indications plus a three-of-nine criteria pathway; the flowchart presents a
   major/minor structure with different thresholds. Which structure should
   govern, or must both coexist?

## Visual/table evidence

3. **Treatment table transcription.** Do the five treatment-context rows
   (alternatives, doses, intervals, durations) match the page 5 table exactly?

4. **Footnote and merged-cell alignment.** Does each footnote modify the exact
   treatment row it appears to annotate?

5. **Risk-factor activation.** Which concrete findings activate the MRSA,
   P. aeruginosa, and resistant-Enterobacterales risk contexts? Is the
   postviral/MRSA context correctly separated?

## Relation semantics

6. **Blood-culture criteria nesting.** Is the structure
   "hospitalized AND (any of BUN/CRP/leukocytes OR at least two of
   DBP/HR/RR/pleuritic pain)" the intended reading?

7. **CT condition grouping.** Is "high probability AND inconclusive radiograph
   AND (diagnostic priority OR suspected complication)" the intended grouping?

8. **Discharge criteria.** Should the discharge paragraph be read as one
   conjunctive eligibility state, or are some items alternatives?

9. **Treatment → discharge.** Does the discharge-criteria assessment follow
   every hospitalized treatment context, including ICU/UCE pathways?

10. **Febrile-neutropenia CT.** Should this imaging context remain independent
    of the radiography pathway, or does the source intend a connection?

11. **Repeat radiograph at 48 hours.** Does this recommendation apply to both
    outpatient and inpatient initial radiographs?

## Review-state hygiene

12. **Blocked candidates.** Should any BLOCKED treatment candidate remain
    blocked, or is source-region verification available to unblock specific
    rows?
