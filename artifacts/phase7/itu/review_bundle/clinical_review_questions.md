# CT-PL-197 v06 (ITU) — Clinical Review Questions

These questions are derived from the open Issues and Phase 5 relation review.
They are for qualified clinical reviewers; no answer is proposed here.

## Source conflicts and scope

1. **Complicated UTI definitions.** Should the structural/functional
   definition, the beyond-bladder definition, or a merged formulation govern
   future knowledge artifacts?

2. **Upper UTI disposition.** Upper UTI is listed as a hospitalization
   indication, yet the treatment table contains "ITU alta ambulatoria". Is the
   outpatient row intentional, an exception, or an editorial error?

3. **Pediatric statement.** The adult-scoped protocol retains a pediatric
   pyelonephritis hospitalization statement. Should it be removed, kept as a
   footnote, or preserved as a separate pediatric pathway?

4. **Oral switch versus discharge.** The criteria list is labelled
   "paso a terapia oral y/o egreso". Are oral transition and discharge the
   same decision, sequential decisions, or independently gated?

## Table alignment

5. **Pregnancy table continuation.** Do the preventive antibiotic options and
   gestational-week restrictions align with the correct rows across pages 4–5?

6. **Footnote applicability.** Do the general-table footnotes (recurrent UTI,
   resistant Gram-negative risk, amikacin/shock, meropenem/shock) modify the
   exact rows they appear to annotate?

7. **Prevention footnote.** Footnote 2 states the preventive decision is based
   on cultures and susceptibility. Does this apply to both the lower- and
   upper-UTI prevention rows?

## Diagnostic exceptions and thresholds

8. **Urinalysis exception.** Is the urinalysis exception (women, first episode,
   typical symptoms) correctly separated from the urine-culture exception
   (premenopausal woman, first uncomplicated lower-UTI episode)?

9. **Culture thresholds.** Are the three positivity thresholds correctly
   scoped to spontaneous urine, symptomatic context, and newly inserted
   catheter samples?

10. **Urgent imaging anchor.** The projection anchors urgent imaging at
    symptomatic UTI (not only classified upper UTI). Is that reading of the
    source correct?

## Relation semantics

11. **Hospitalization list.** Are the hospitalization bullets alternatives
    (OR) as modelled?

12. **Transition list.** Should the oral-step/discharge criteria be one
    conjunctive state with oxygen saturation OR PaO2 nested, or another
    grouping?

13. **Culture-guided adjustment.** Are the BRANCH relations from every
    treatment rule to culture/antibiogram adjustment the intended flow, and
    should prevention rules participate?

14. **Gram guiding role.** Does the urine Gram stain inform every empirical
    treatment context as modelled, or only specific ones?

15. **Resistant Gram-negative risk.** Are the risk-factor activations
    (hospitalization >48 h in 3 months, antibiotics in 90 days, ESBL
    colonization) complete for this institutional protocol?

## Review-state hygiene

16. **Blocked candidates.** Should any BLOCKED candidate (outpatient upper UTI
    branch, prevention rows) remain blocked, or is evidence available to
    adjudicate them?
